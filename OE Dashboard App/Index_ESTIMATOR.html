Leaderboard
How to read this: Bids are counted by Submitted date (fallback: Bid Deadline, then Created date) with value = Submitted $. Awards are counted by Awarded date with value = Won Quotes $. Windows are calendar-based and live through today: Today = today's activity · Weekly = Monday through today · Monthly = 1st through today · Q1–Q4 = calendar quarters (current quarter runs through today) · YTD = Jan 1 through today · T12 = trailing 12 full months plus month-to-date. Note this differs from the Bid Funnel Overview, which anchors on yesterday — same-day figures here may still be filling in as the team works. Leaderboard split: New Bids = submitted in the window AND created (col C) in FY2026; Rebids = submitted in the window but created before 2026 — carryover pipeline being repriced or repushed. Cohort Win % and the flag threshold still use the combined total (new + rebid), since awards can come from either. Avg New Bid covers new bids only. In the name-cell bar, solid gray = new $, darker gray = rebid $, red = awarded $.
Two win rates, on purpose. The overview Decided Win % \u00b7 Created FY row is Won $ \u00f7 (Won $ + Lost $) among this year's created bids that have actually reached a decision — undecided bids are excluded, so it answers "of what's been decided, did we win our share?" and is meaningful mid-year. Wins are dated by Awarded date with Won Quotes $; losses by Lost date, valued at Lost Quotes $ (falling back to Submitted $ where Lost Quotes wasn't entered — currently a meaningful share of losses, a hygiene item). Two caveats: early in the year it over-represents fast-deciding small work, and dollar-weighted will read below count-based whenever we win small and lose big — the count version is shown underneath for exactly that comparison. The leaderboard's Cohort Win % column is different by design: all vintages submitted in the window followed to eventual outcome — the mature, defensible conversion number for assessing books (20%+ at full maturity).
Sales Cycle = days from Created date (col C — when the opportunity first entered BasisBoard) to Awarded date. Revenue Cycle = days from Created date to OE Start Date (col AO) — when revenue generation begins. The gap between the two is the award-to-billing lag. Both are measured on awards landed in the window, use medians (not averages) so one outlier can't skew them, and split at $750K award value to match division structure. Cycles below 0 or above 2,000 days are excluded as entry noise; awards missing an OE Start Date drop out of Revenue Cycle only. OE Start Dates can be scheduled in the future, so recent-window Revenue Cycles include projected starts. The count next to each leaderboard Med Cycle figure is the awards behind the median — treat medians on fewer than ~5 awards as anecdote.
Capacity matrix: monthly bid volume per estimator, shaded to each row's own trailing-12 peak. Baselines are per-estimator by design — books differ (Special Projects estimators carry many small bids; enterprise estimators few large ones), so compare an estimator to their own baseline, not to each other. Sustained volume above baseline across the team, alongside capacity declines in the Bid Funnel, is the signal that estimating headcount is the constraint.
Individual-estimator win rate is context, not a standalone KPI — portfolio mix and CE bid selection drive it. Ratios on fewer than 10 bids show an asterisk. Dollar metrics lead throughout; counts are secondary. Data reads live from BasisBoard's sheet on every page load.
